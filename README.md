# 📊 Supply-Demand & Profitability Dashboard (Power BI)

## Insigh BI — Data Visualization for Business Optimization

A Power BI dashboard project analyzing supply chain and sales performance data to uncover the relationship between demand, availability, and profitability.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project visualizes key business metrics to help stakeholders understand the drivers behind profit and loss trends. It focuses on evaluating daily product demand against availability, quantifying the financial impact of supply shortages, and surfacing insights that support better inventory and demand planning decisions.

**Tool used:** Power BI Desktop

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze supply chain and sales performance data using Power BI to identify the key drivers behind profit and loss trends. The dashboard evaluates the relationship between daily product demand and availability, quantifies the impact of supply shortages on overall business performance, and provides actionable insights to help stakeholders optimize inventory planning, reduce average daily losses, and improve profitability.

**Key goals:**
- Analyze total profit vs. total loss to assess overall business performance
- Track average daily loss to identify recurring inefficiencies
- Compare average daily demand against average daily availability to detect supply-demand gaps
- Quantify total supply shortage and its contribution to financial loss
- Provide a consolidated dashboard for stakeholders to monitor performance and support planning decisions

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Profit | 301K |
| Total Loss | 8M |
| Average Daily Loss | 2.97K |
| Average Demand per Day | 48.65 |
| Average Availability per Day | 24.70 |
| Total Supply Shortage | 61K |

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Loss significantly outweighs profit** — Total Loss (8M) is substantially higher than Total Profit (301K), indicating the business is currently operating at a net deficit.
- **Consistent daily losses** — An Average Daily Loss of 2.97K shows losses are a recurring pattern rather than isolated incidents, pointing to systemic issues in supply, pricing, or forecasting.
- **Demand consistently outpaces availability** — Average Demand per Day (48.65) is nearly double the Average Availability per Day (24.70), highlighting a persistent supply-demand imbalance.
- **Supply shortage is a major contributor to loss** — A Total Supply Shortage of 61K units reflects the cumulative effect of insufficient stock, directly correlating with the high total loss figure.
- **Availability is the bottleneck, not demand** — Since demand remains strong and stable, the core issue lies in inventory/supply chain execution.
- **Opportunity for optimization** — Bridging the demand-availability gap could meaningfully reduce average daily loss and improve overall profitability.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Improve inventory forecasting to better align stock levels with daily demand
- Reduce supplier lead times to minimize stockouts and shortages
- Investigate high-loss periods/products to identify root causes
- Set up automated alerts for when availability drops significantly below demand
- Continuously monitor the demand-availability gap as a leading indicator of future loss
